# Project Feedback Video Script (about 60–90 seconds)

Hello, my name is Purva Yogesh Pawar, and this is my project, “Cost Structure and Channel-Wise Profitability Analysis for Multi-Channel Restaurants.”

The goal of this project is to understand why revenue alone is not enough to evaluate a restaurant sales channel. Different channels may have different food, labor, packaging, platform-fee, overhead and discount costs.

I used Python, pandas, NumPy, Plotly and Streamlit. The project includes a structured CSV dataset, calculations for total cost, modeled profit and profit margin, and an interactive dashboard. The dashboard compares dine-in, online delivery, takeaway and aggregator delivery, displays cost composition, and shows monthly revenue and profit trends.

One important limitation is that the included data is synthetic and created for demonstration. It should not be treated as real restaurant financial data. With validated operational data, this workflow could help managers review channel contribution, pricing, discounts and operating costs.

Through this project I practiced data preparation, aggregation, visualization, dashboard development and communicating analytical limitations. Thank you.
